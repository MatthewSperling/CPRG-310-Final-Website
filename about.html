My Story

I’m Matt Sperling, founder of Night Sparrow Productions, based in Calgary, Alberta. My passion for audio, video, and creative media led me to enroll in SAIT’s Interactive Design Diploma (IDD) program, where I honed skills in web development, UI/UX, graphic design, and storytelling. Working alongside peers like Neil Capistrano and Chloe Sueun Choi, I built our first real-world collaboration: the Sparrow Productions website—learning the power of teamwork and technical craftsmanship. Parallel to my studies, I volunteered for over a year with the Women in Need Society (WINS), producing interviews, promotional videos, and event coverage that combined empathy with design thinking. Projects like Alla’s journey from Ukraine and Alphonsine’s transformation through WINS programs taught me how intentional storytelling can amplify voices and drive real community impact. Now, I’m channeling these experiences into the future of Night Sparrow Productions—a Calgary-based multimedia agency that seamlessly integrates video, photography, audio, and web development to help nonprofits and small businesses share their stories. By leveraging empathy, design thinking, and collaborative creativity, I aim to expand our service offerings, forge new partnerships, and create content that leaves a lasting impression. My Mission

My mission is to empower nonprofits, small businesses, and individuals in Calgary to share their unique stories through high-quality multimedia content—video, photography, audio, and web design. Grounded in empathy and design thinking, I commit to deeply listening to each client’s vision, iterating collaboratively, and crafting visuals that resonate across audiences. By delivering thoughtfully designed, impactful content, I strive to foster genuine connections, drive community impact, and leave a lasting impression.

Our Collaborations

We are proud to have partnered with organizations like WINS (Women in Need Society) to produce impactful videos that raise awareness and foster positive change. Together, we tell stories that matter.
